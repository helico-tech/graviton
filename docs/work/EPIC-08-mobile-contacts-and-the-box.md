# EPIC-08 Mobile contacts and the uncertainty box

The design's centre of gravity (GAME-0001 §2, §4.8; domain doc §3-4; ADR-0005
"Reachable set", "Confidence"): free-flying contacts under stuck-open thrust
with an avoidance reflex, observed late like everything else; the amber
uncertainty box the staleness implies, the ice-blue reachable set the probe's
remaining budget implies, their overlap and the confidence number in the
SOLUTION panel; and the first campaign beat where the box appears (beat 7,
Staleness), solved and verified. Done when the two ellipses are on the plot for
a mobile contact, `confidence` is a readout, and level 07 replays in CI.

Conditional clauses (beat 8), relays and observers, exposure and debris are
later epics; the box here is the one-observer, circular v1 of research 02 §6.4.
